A mixed container that respects the shelf and the label.

Wholesale buyers in the Middle East often need mixed SKUs, Arabic labels and a container that is loaded to a plan. We coordinate the suppliers, check the labels and consolidate the load in Yiwu.

Send your requirement See how it works

A mixed container is a puzzle: carton sizes, SKU counts, label languages and a loading plan that has to survive the port. Getting the goods is only half of it; the other half is making sure the right carton carries the right mark.

We coordinate the Yiwu side — suppliers, labels, QC and loading — so your buyer receives a container that matches the packing list.

What we handle

  • Multi-supplier sourcing and receiving
  • Arabic / English label coordination
  • Mixed cartons and carton marks
  • QC and quantity checks
  • Container loading plan
  • Export document coordination

What to send

  • SKU list with quantities and carton specs
  • Label artwork and language requirements
  • Target volume and destination port
  • Shipping deadline

How the job runs

  1. We confirm the SKU list, carton plan and label requirements.
  2. Goods are received and checked against the PO.
  3. Labels are applied and cartons are marked to the plan.
  4. The container is loaded and photographed before departure.

Good to know

  • Label translation and regulatory approval stay with you.
  • We confirm carton dimensions before loading.
  • Container photos and the loading sequence are shared before departure.

Home/Southeast Asia e-commerce restocking

Restock runs that fit a fast e-commerce calendar.

Southeast Asian sellers rarely need a full container. They need a repeatable restock: labels, barcodes, small batches and a shipping schedule that matches the campaign. We handle the Yiwu side so your team can focus on sell-through.

Send your requirement See how it works

E-commerce restocking is a rhythm, not a one-off purchase. The first order is a test; the second order is the real spec. If the label, barcode or pack-out changes between runs, the warehouse feels it.

We lock the spec after the first run and reuse it. Reorders become a quantity change, not a new project.

What we handle

  • Small-batch sourcing and receiving
  • Barcode and label application
  • Poly bagging and protective packing
  • Carton marks and mixed SKU cartons
  • Air and sea consolidation options
  • Repeat-order spec retention

What to send

  • SKU list and quantities
  • Barcode and label files
  • Restock date and destination
  • Any marketplace inbound requirements

How the job runs

  1. We confirm the SKU list and the inbound requirements.
  2. Goods are received, labeled and checked.
  3. We pack to the carton plan and consolidate.
  4. The approved spec is retained for the next restock.

Good to know

  • We quote air and sea separately so you can choose the trade-off.
  • Barcode verification is part of QC.
  • Reorders can reuse the approved spec without re-quoting the setup.

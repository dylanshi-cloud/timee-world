A private-label launch is a chain of small dependencies: the bottle arrives, the carton is printed, the label is approved, the batch code is applied, and the set has to be packed in the right order. One missing step holds the whole run.

We act as the Yiwu side of that chain. You keep control of the formula and the regulatory copy; we coordinate the packaging, labeling and assembly around your components and your deadline.

What we handle

  • Folding cartons and rigid boxes
  • Pressure-sensitive labels and shrink sleeves
  • Batch and expiry code application
  • Inserts, leaflets and thank-you cards
  • Shrink wrap and tamper seals
  • Assembly and shelf-ready packing

What to send

  • Final artwork and dielines
  • Bottle, jar or tube specifications
  • Label copy and batch/expiry format
  • Quantity and destination market

How the job runs

  1. We check the artwork against the component and flag fit or print issues.
  2. You approve a digital proof; a physical proof is optional.
  3. Labels and cartons are produced and checked against the approved proof.
  4. We assemble, code, inspect and consolidate the finished units.

Good to know

  • You approve final label copy and are responsible for regulatory compliance in the destination market.
  • Batch coding and expiry dates need a confirmed format before production.
  • We can work with your existing components or help source them.

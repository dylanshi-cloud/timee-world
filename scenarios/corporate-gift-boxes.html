Home/Corporate & promotional gift boxes

Corporate gifts that arrive as a set, not a pile of boxes.

A branded gift box has to look consistent across every unit and arrive before the event. We coordinate printing, assembly, labeling and carton packing so your team hands over a finished set.

Send your requirement See how it works

Corporate gift projects are deadline projects. The event date does not move, and a box that arrives two days late is a box that stays in the storage room.

We start from the deadline and work backward: what can be printed, what can be assembled, and what has to be substituted if a component is late. You get a clear plan, not a hopeful promise.

What we handle

  • Printed gift boxes and sleeves
  • Logo printing and finishing
  • Insert cards and labels
  • Assembly of multi-item sets
  • Individual packing and master cartons
  • Delivery-ready carton marks

What to send

  • Logo and brand guidelines
  • Gift components and quantities
  • Event date and destination
  • Any packing or presentation reference

How the job runs

  1. We confirm the deadline and build a production plan backward from it.
  2. You approve the box and assembly mockup.
  3. We print, assemble and label the sets.
  4. Sets are packed for delivery and consolidated.

Good to know

  • Start with the deadline; it determines which processes are realistic.
  • We can assemble mixed sets with different SKUs.
  • Pre-production samples are recommended for logo and color approval.

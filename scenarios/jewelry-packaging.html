Home/Jewelry & accessory packaging

Jewelry packaging that makes a small order look established.

A ring card, a pouch, a box and a thank-you note can carry more brand weight than the product photo. We print, assemble and label jewelry packaging in small runs so you can test a collection without ordering a warehouse full.

Send your requirement See how it works

Jewelry buyers see the packaging before they see the piece. The card stock, the pouch, the seal and the insert all say something about the brand — and inconsistent packaging makes even a good product feel temporary.

We work with jewelry brands, accessory sellers and buying agents who need a controlled run: the same card, the same label, the same pack, repeated across SKUs. Start with a reference or a dieline; we will quote the smallest sensible run and build from there.

What we handle

  • Printed backing cards and earring cards
  • Pouches, envelopes and small folding boxes
  • Stickers, seals and thank-you cards
  • Barcode and SKU label application
  • Assembly and poly bagging
  • Mixed-SKU cartons for one shipment

What to send

  • Your logo and any artwork you already have
  • Dimensions, a dieline, or a photo of the current pack
  • Quantity per SKU and the number of SKUs
  • Destination country and target date

How the job runs

  1. We review the pack and confirm what can be printed, matched or assembled.
  2. You approve a digital mockup before anything is produced.
  3. A sample is made if the material or finish needs a physical check.
  4. We produce, inspect and consolidate the run for shipment.

Good to know

  • Low minimums are realistic for labels, cards and assembly; printed boxes depend on material and finishing.
  • Paper colors can be matched to Pantone. Fabric, foil and screen colors may shift slightly between runs.
  • Mixed-SKU cartons are fine. Tell us how you want the cartons marked.

Home/Amazon FBA prep & labeling

FBA prep that survives the inbound check.

FNSKU labels, poly bagging, suffocation warnings, carton marks and mixed-SKU boxes are not hard — they are just easy to get wrong. We prep your units in Yiwu before they leave, so the first scan happens at the warehouse, not in a returns report.

Send your requirement See how it works

Amazon sellers lose more time to inbound rejections than to sourcing. A missing FNSKU, a carton over the weight limit, or a poly bag without the warning label can turn a good shipment into a week of back-and-forth.

We prep against your shipment plan: label, bag, mark, weigh and check. If something does not match the plan, we send photos before the cartons are sealed.

What we handle

  • FNSKU label application and verification
  • Poly bagging and suffocation warning labels
  • Bubble wrap and protective packing
  • Carton marks and shipment labels
  • Carton weight and dimension checks
  • Mixed-SKU cartons and pallet preparation

What to send

  • FBA shipment plan and SKU list
  • FNSKU or label file
  • Units per carton and carton limits
  • Destination fulfillment center

How the job runs

  1. We check the label file and the shipment plan against the units received.
  2. Units are labeled, bagged and packed to the carton plan.
  3. We verify labels, weights, dimensions and carton marks.
  4. Cartons are sealed and consolidated for the inbound shipment.

Good to know

  • You provide the FNSKU or label file; we apply and verify it.
  • We check carton weight and dimensions against your plan, not against a guess.
  • We can consolidate multiple suppliers into one inbound shipment.

Home/Shopify & DTC inserts

Packaging that makes the unboxing worth filming.

For a DTC brand, the box is the first physical touchpoint. We print mailer boxes, cards and stickers, assemble inserts and pack orders in low volumes so you can iterate on the creative without writing off thousands of units.

Send your requirement See how it works

DTC brands test fast. A packaging run that takes six months and a five-figure minimum kills the test before it starts. The better path is a small first run, a real unboxing, and a reorder once the creative proves itself.

We build around that rhythm: digital mockups first, low-volume printing where the process allows it, and assembly that can change when the campaign changes.

What we handle

  • Mailer boxes and shipping boxes
  • Thank-you cards and insert cards
  • Stickers, seals and tissue paper
  • Custom tape and packing slips
  • Kitting and poly bagging
  • Direct-to-3PL or direct-to-customer packing

What to send

  • Brand assets and any existing artwork
  • A dieline, reference box or photo
  • Quantity per SKU and insert copy
  • Destination: 3PL, warehouse or customer

How the job runs

  1. We turn your reference into a digital mockup for approval.
  2. You confirm copy, color and pack-out.
  3. We print and assemble the first run.
  4. We pack to your 3PL or order plan and consolidate.

Good to know

  • Digital mockups let you approve the look before printing.
  • Short runs are best for testing; reorders get faster once the spec is locked.
  • We can ship to your 3PL or pack individual customer orders.

What we handle

You keep the buyer relationship. We run the Yiwu side: receiving, checking, marking and consolidating.

  • Multi-supplier receiving and quantity checks
  • QC against your checklist
  • Mixed-SKU cartons and carton marks
  • Label and barcode verification
  • Consolidation and container loading plans
  • Export document coordination

Why not just use a forwarder?

A forwarder moves boxes. It does not open them, count them, check the label or tell you that one supplier sent the old version. That work has to happen before consolidation, or the buyer discovers it at the destination.

What you send

  • Supplier list and contact details
  • PO / SKU list with quantities
  • QC standard and label requirements
  • Shipping deadline and destination

How the job runs

  1. We receive each supplier's goods and check quantity and condition.
  2. QC is run against your standard; issues are photographed.
  3. We consolidate into the carton plan and apply marks.
  4. The shipment is released with a packing list and photos.

Good to know

  • We report shortages and defects before consolidation, not after.
  • Your buyer relationship stays yours; factory details stay private.
  • We can work under your brand or as a white-label back office.

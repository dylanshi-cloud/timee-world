What we handle

New product development is a sequence of small decisions. We help you make them in the right order, before tooling money is spent.

  • Feasibility review against a reference or drawing
  • Material and process selection
  • Tooling and prototype coordination
  • Packaging and label development
  • Pilot run and production handover

What we need

  • A drawing, sketch, sample or reference product
  • Target price and target quantity
  • Target market and any certification requirement
  • Packaging and branding expectations

What we won't promise

We won't quote a tooling cost or a production date before the factory has seen the drawing. A number invented at the first call becomes a problem at the first invoice. We confirm the scope, then quote.

How the job runs

  1. We review the reference and identify the open questions.
  2. The factory quotes tooling and sampling against the drawing.
  3. You approve the prototype and the packaging direction.
  4. A pilot run checks the process before full production.
  5. Production and QC run against the approved spec.

Good to know

  • Tooling is quoted separately and owned according to the factory agreement.
  • Certification requirements should be raised before the prototype, not after.
  • A pilot run is cheaper than discovering a process problem in a full container.

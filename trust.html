One accountable contact

You talk to one person from quote to container. When a supplier is late, a label is wrong or a carton is over weight, you get one answer and one plan — not five versions of the truth.

QC before consolidation

We inspect against an agreed checklist before the goods are consolidated. The checklist is shared with the quote, so you know what will be checked before production starts.

CheckWhat it covers
QuantityUnits received vs. PO, per SKU
Artwork & colorPrint against the approved proof

Milestone payments

Typically 30% deposit and the balance before shipment. The exact terms are confirmed on the quote. We do not add charges after the fact; if the scope changes, we quote the change first.

Factory details stay private

We can sign an NDA and keep factory names and pricing behind us. This protects your sourcing position and keeps the supplier relationship stable. If you need a factory audit or a certification document, we coordinate it without publishing the factory name.

What we will not do

  • Publish your factory, your client list or your pricing
  • Ship without your approval when a spec is out of tolerance
  • Quote a price we cannot hold
  • Use your artwork or designs outside your order

What we ask from you

  • A clear spec, or enough reference to build one
  • A target quantity and destination
  • One decision-maker for approvals
  • A realistic target date

Ask for the QC checklist

Send your product and destination; we will share the checklist we would use for your order.

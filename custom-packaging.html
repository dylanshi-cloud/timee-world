What we handle

We work from your artwork, dieline, sample or reference. If you only have a logo and a product, we can start there and build the pack around it.

  • Folding cartons, mailer boxes and rigid boxes
  • Pressure-sensitive labels, stickers and seals
  • Backing cards, insert cards and thank-you cards
  • Stand-up pouches and flat pouches
  • Foil, emboss, spot UV, die-cut and window patching
  • Kitting, poly bagging and assembly

What you send

  • Logo and any print-ready artwork you already have
  • A dieline, a sample, or a photo of the pack you want to match
  • Quantity per SKU and the number of SKUs
  • Material and finish preference, if you have one
  • Destination country and target date

How the job runs

  1. We review the brief and confirm what can be printed, matched or assembled.
  2. You approve a digital mockup before anything is produced.
  3. A physical sample is made when the material or finish needs a hand check.
  4. Production runs against the approved spec, then QC checks the output.
  5. We consolidate the cartons and release the shipment with photos.

What affects price

Material, print method, size, quantity, finishing and assembly time. We quote the trade-offs instead of hiding them in one number: a heavier board, a foil stamp or a hand-assembled insert each change the cost in a different way.

Good to know

  • Low minimums are realistic for labels, cards and assembly; printed boxes depend on material and finishing.
  • Paper colors can be matched to Pantone. Fabric, foil and screen colors may shift slightly between runs.
  • Mixed-SKU cartons are fine. Tell us how you want the cartons marked.

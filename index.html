A clear path from requirement to container.

You don't need to know our process before you contact us. Send what you have; we'll tell you what is missing.

01

Send the requirement

Product, quantity, customization, destination and target date. A photo or link is enough to start.

02

Get a 48-hour quote

We confirm what is possible, what is missing and what the trade-offs are. No price without a scope.

03

Approve a sample or mockup

Digital mockup first. A physical sample when the material or finish needs to be checked by hand.

04

Produce and inspect

Production runs against the approved spec. QC checks quantity, artwork, labels, barcodes and packing.

05

Consolidate and ship

We mark the cartons, consolidate the load and release it with photos and a packing list.

What we need from you

  • Product, photo, sample or reference link
  • Quantity per SKU and number of SKUs
  • What needs customizing: packaging, label, color, assembly, barcode
  • Destination country or port, and target date

What you get back

  • A quote with scope, assumptions and lead time
  • A digital mockup before production
  • A QC checklist and photos before consolidation
  • Shipping options: FOB, CIF or DDP where available

Questions buyers ask before the first quote.

Short answers. The full list is on the FAQ page.

What is your minimum order?

It depends on the process. Labels, cards and assembly can start small; printed packaging and tooling have higher minimums. Tell us the quantity you need and we will confirm before you commit.

How fast can you quote?

Within 48 hours on business days, once we have the product, quantity, customization and destination. If the scope is incomplete, we will ask for the missing piece instead of quoting a guess.

Can you match my existing packaging?

Yes. Send a sample, dieline, photo or link. We will quote a match and send a digital mockup before production.

Will you tell me which factory you use?

No. We are your single point of contact, and factory names and pricing stay behind us. We can sign an NDA and share the records you need to approve the shipment.

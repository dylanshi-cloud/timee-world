A quote in 48 hours. No black box after that.

Five steps from your first message to a consolidated shipment. You approve each stage before we move.

Send your requirement See how it works

1. Send the requirement

Use the RFQ form or email us. The minimum is product, quantity, customization, destination and target date. A photo, link or sample photo helps more than a long description.

2. Get a 48-hour quote

We confirm what is possible, what is missing and what the trade-offs are. If the scope is incomplete, we ask for the missing piece instead of quoting a guess. The quote states the scope, assumptions and lead time.

3. Approve a sample or mockup

Digital mockup first. A physical sample when the material, finish or fit needs to be checked by hand. Sampling is quoted separately and confirmed before it starts.

4. Produce and inspect

Production runs against the approved spec. QC checks quantity, artwork, label placement, barcode scan, carton marks and packing. If something is out of spec, we send photos and hold the shipment for your decision.

5. Consolidate and ship

We mark the cartons, consolidate the load and release it with photos and a packing list. Shipping is quoted FOB, CIF or DDP where available, depending on the destination.

What we need from you

  • Product, photo, sample or reference link
  • Quantity per SKU and number of SKUs
  • What needs customizing: packaging, label, color, assembly, barcode
  • Destination country or port, and target date

What you get back

  • A quote with scope, assumptions and lead time
  • A digital mockup before production
  • A QC checklist and photos before consolidation
  • Shipping options: FOB, CIF or DDP where available

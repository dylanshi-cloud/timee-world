What is your minimum order?

It depends on the process. Labels, cards and assembly can start small; printed packaging and tooling have higher minimums. Tell us the quantity you need and we will confirm before you commit.

How fast can you quote?

Within 48 hours on business days, once we have the product, quantity, customization and destination. If the scope is incomplete, we will ask for the missing piece instead of quoting a guess.

Can you match my existing packaging?

Yes. Send a sample, dieline, photo or link. We will quote a match and send a digital mockup before production.

Do you work with Amazon sellers?

Yes. FNSKU labels, poly bagging, carton marks and FBA prep are part of the service. Send your shipment plan and label file with the RFQ.

Can you handle multiple suppliers?

Yes. We receive from several suppliers, check the goods, consolidate and ship one mixed carton or container.

Will you disclose my factory?

No. We are your single point of contact. Factory names and pricing stay behind us, and we can sign an NDA.

What about samples?

Sampling is quoted separately. Sample cost and lead time depend on the process; we confirm both before starting.

What payment terms do you use?

Typically 30% deposit and the balance before shipment. Terms are confirmed on the quote, and any scope change is quoted before it is produced.

What if the goods are wrong?

We inspect against the agreed checklist before consolidation. If something is out of spec, we send photos and hold the shipment for your decision.

Do you ship worldwide?

We quote FOB, CIF or DDP depending on the destination. Tell us the market and we will include the right option.

Still have a question?

Send it with your product and quantity. We will answer in writing, not with a sales call.
